CORE PAYROLL FUNCTIONALITY
Dayforce is built on a single-database, continuous-calculation architecture. Payroll
recalculates in real time as HR and time data changes — there is no "payroll run" batch;
administrators review and approve the continuously updated payroll register.

Pay frequencies and employee types:
- All frequencies in a single tenant: weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, monthly, and ad hoc
- All employee classifications unified in single PEPM: exempt, non-exempt, faculty
  (9/10/12-month), graduate assistants, student workers, casuals — no separate tiers or modules
- Multiple pay rates and positions per employee, unlimited
- Faculty spread pay (9-month salary spread over 12 months): natively supported
- Graduate assistant stipend processing: supported without add-on module
- Retroactive pay: fully automated across all pay periods and classifications
- On-demand pay / earned wage access: available as add-on ($2.50 PEPM, Dayforce Wallet)

Garnishments:
- Child support, tax levies, creditor garnishments, student loans, bankruptcy
- Automated priority calculation and disposable income limits
- All garnishment types included in base PEPM; no per-transaction fee
- Ceridian registers and manages state income withholding orders

Tax filing (all 50 states + federal + local; included in base PEPM):
- Federal: 940, 941, W-2, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, ACA 1095-C
- Multi-state: all states included at no per-state surcharge
- Local payroll tax: all applicable local jurisdictions filed automatically
- New hire reporting: automated for all states
- Ceridian bears full liability for penalties caused by Ceridian errors, no dollar cap

Employee self-service (Dayforce Mobile + Web):
- Pay stub access: current and full history (no year limit)
- W-2 and 1099 electronic delivery; prior-year access from self-service
- Direct deposit: unlimited accounts, instant update without payroll cycle delay
- W-4 and state withholding: updates effective immediately in payroll
- Full feature parity between iOS app, Android app, and web portal
- Accessible via personal email for employees without institutional credentials
- Dayforce Wallet (on-demand pay add-on): employees access earned wages before pay date
