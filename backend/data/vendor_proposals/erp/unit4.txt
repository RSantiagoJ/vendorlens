CORE ERP FUNCTIONALITY
Unit4 ERP for Education is designed specifically for higher education, with pre-built
configurations for GASB, 2 CFR 200, NACUBO, and multi-campus operations. The platform
ships with a Higher Education Foundation layer — a set of pre-built financial structures,
chart of accounts templates, and reporting packages tailored to the sector.

Financial Management:
- General ledger: purpose-built for higher education fund accounting (GASB 34, 87, 96)
  Pre-built GASB financial statement templates included (no configuration required)
  Unrestricted, restricted, and permanently restricted fund structures pre-configured
- AP, AR, and expense management included
- Budget management: position-based budgeting, project budgeting, encumbrance accounting
- Federal grant management: 2 CFR 200 / Uniform Guidance natively built and
  pre-configured; indirect cost rate management, effort reporting, and invoicing included
  Unit4 has deployed at multiple NSF and NIH grant-intensive research universities
- Fixed assets with depreciation and impairment
- Procurement: purchase requisitions, POs, supplier management, three-way matching
- Endowment: distribution calculation and spending policy management (pre-built)
- NACUBO reporting: standard templates included; annual NACUBO survey export automated

Human Resources and Payroll:
- Core HR: employee records, position management, org hierarchy
- US Payroll: all states, all employee types, all frequencies (no per-state surcharge)
- Faculty-specific: appointment types (tenure track, adjunct, visiting), course load,
  9/10/12-month spread pay, sabbatical leave management — all native, no add-on
- Graduate assistant: stipend, tuition waiver, and fee remission tracking natively supported
- Student workers: full support including I-9 management
- Benefits: open enrollment, life events, ACA 1095-C (included, no add-on)
- Time and attendance: Unit4 Time included; configurable for departmental overtime rules
- Retroactive pay: fully automated for all employee types and pay periods
- On-demand pay: not yet available; targeted for 2027 release
