CORE ERP FUNCTIONALITY
Workday Financials is a unified accounting and financial management platform.
Workday HCM combines HR, payroll, benefits, talent, and time in a single data model.

Financial Management:
- General ledger with unlimited worktag dimensions (replaces chart of accounts)
- Accounts payable and accounts receivable
- Budget development, monitoring, and variance reporting
- Multi-entity and multi-fund accounting: operating, capital, plant, endowment, grants
- Federal grant management: 2 CFR 200 / Uniform Guidance native; sponsored programs tracking
- GASB financial reporting: Workday has published GASB 87, 96 configuration guides;
  GASB compliance requires specific configuration; out-of-box is not pre-configured for GASB
- Fixed assets: asset capitalization, depreciation, and disposal
- Procurement: purchase orders, vendor master, contract lifecycle management
- Expense management: employee expense reports, receipt capture, policy enforcement
- Cash and treasury: bank reconciliation, cash forecasting; advanced treasury not included

Human Resources and Payroll:
- Full HCM: position management, compensation, benefits, talent, succession
- Payroll (US): all employee types, all frequencies; continuous payroll calculation model
- Faculty workload management: course load tracking, effort reporting in development
- Graduate assistant and student worker support: requires Workday Student or custom config
  (Workday Student is a separate, additional subscription; without it, student worker
  management requires manual workaround for academic-year appointment tracking)
- Time tracking: Workday Time (included); integration with badging systems via partner
- Retro pay: automated; configurable retro pay rules per compensation plan
