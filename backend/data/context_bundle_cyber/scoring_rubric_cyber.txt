VENDORLENS SCORING RUBRIC
Source: Higher Education Managed Cybersecurity Services RFP
Scale: 0–10 per dimension. All scores rounded to one decimal place.
Weights sum to 1.00. Maximum possible overall score is 10.0.

Overall score = weighted sum of all dimension scores × their weights (defined in rfp_criteria_cyber.txt).

---

CORE SECURITY CAPABILITY (weight: 0.25)
Dimension identifier: core_lms_functionality

Evaluate: 24x7 SOC staffing, SIEM capabilities, threat detection coverage,
vulnerability management, penetration testing, threat intelligence, phishing simulation

10 = Full 24x7 SOC with tiered analysts. SIEM with real-time correlation. Complete
     vulnerability management program. Annual pen testing included in base price.
     Threat intelligence integrated. Phishing simulation platform included.
     Dark web monitoring included. All required functionality documented and priced.

7-9 = Core SOC and SIEM present. Most required capabilities documented. Minor gaps
     (e.g., pen testing available as add-on, phishing simulation extra cost,
     or threat intelligence feeds limited to one major provider).

4-6 = Basic SOC monitoring present. Significant gaps: no integrated vulnerability
     management, pen testing not offered directly, or SIEM requires customer-managed
     infrastructure. Detection limited to signature-based approaches.

1-3 = Minimal detection capability. SOC hours limited (not 24x7). Major required
     services absent or subcontracted without disclosure.

0 = Core security operations absent or critically underdocumented.

---

REGULATORY FRAMEWORK COMPLIANCE (weight: 0.15)
Dimension identifier: accessibility_compliance

Evaluate: SOC 2 Type II currency, ISO 27001, FedRAMP authorization,
NIST CSF alignment, FERPA/GLBA/applicable state data privacy law documentation

10 = Current SOC 2 Type II (within 18 months) AND ISO 27001 certified.
     FedRAMP Moderate or High authorization. NIST CSF alignment documented.
     Complete FERPA, GLBA, and applicable state data privacy compliance package provided.

7-9 = SOC 2 Type II current. ISO 27001 in progress or alternative equivalent.
     NIST CSF alignment documented. FERPA/GLBA compliance addressed. FedRAMP
     in process or not applicable to on-premises components.

4-6 = SOC 2 Type II present but aging (18-24 months). ISO 27001 not certified.
     Regulatory compliance documentation partial or requires follow-up requests.

1-3 = SOC 2 Type I only, or SOC 2 Type II expired (>24 months). No ISO 27001.
     Regulatory compliance documentation missing or insufficient.

0 = No current security certifications. No compliance documentation provided.

---

INTEGRATION & INTEROPERABILITY (weight: 0.10)
Dimension identifier: integration_interoperability

Evaluate: SIEM/SOAR integration, IAM platform support, ticketing integration,
cloud platform coverage (AWS/Azure/GCP), API access, EDR/XDR interoperability

10 = Native integration with major SIEM platforms (Splunk, Sentinel). Full cloud
     coverage (AWS, Azure, GCP). IAM integration (AD, Azure AD, Okta). Ticketing
     via ServiceNow/Jira. Open API documented. EDR/XDR interoperability confirmed.

7-9 = Integration with at least two major SIEM platforms. Cloud coverage for primary
     platforms. API available. IAM integration present. Minor gaps in EDR/XDR interop.

4-6 = Limited SIEM integration (one platform or requires custom connector).
     Partial cloud coverage. API available but not fully documented.

1-3 = Proprietary platform with limited third-party integration. Customer responsible
     for building connectors. Cloud coverage limited to one provider.

0 = No documented integration capability.

---

PRICING & COST TRANSPARENCY (weight: 0.10)
Dimension identifier: pricing_and_licensing

Evaluate: Retainer fee disclosure, per-incident pricing, add-on service costs,
multi-year escalation, overage fees

10 = Full retainer cost disclosed with itemized scope. Per-incident response and
     forensic pricing clearly stated. Add-on service menu with pricing. Multi-year
     terms with capped escalation (≤3% annually). No hidden overage fees.

7-9 = Retainer and most per-incident pricing disclosed. Minor gaps in add-on pricing
     or escalation clause not fully capped. Overall pricing model clear.

4-6 = Base retainer disclosed. Per-incident pricing vague or requires negotiation.
     Escalation clause present but not capped. Add-on pricing not disclosed.

1-3 = Pricing requires significant follow-up. Major cost components undisclosed
     or presented as "upon request" without ranges.

0 = No pricing information provided.

---

VENDOR SECURITY POSTURE (weight: 0.20)
Dimension identifier: security_and_compliance

Evaluate: Vendor's own certifications, pen test cadence, bug bounty,
supply chain security, data residency controls

10 = Vendor holds current SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 for their own operations.
     Annual internal pen testing with remediation summary available.
     Bug bounty or responsible disclosure program active.
     Supply chain security program documented. Data residency options available.

7-9 = SOC 2 Type II current. ISO 27001 in progress. Pen test summary available.
     Responsible disclosure policy in place. Supply chain controls documented.

4-6 = SOC 2 Type II current but vendor security posture documentation limited.
     Pen testing cadence not annual or summary not available. Bug bounty absent.

1-3 = Vendor's own security posture underdocumented. SOC 2 Type II not current.
     No pen test evidence. Supply chain risk unaddressed.

0 = No vendor security posture documentation provided.

INCIDENT RESPONSE SLA & TRAINING (weight: 0.10)
Dimension identifier: support_and_training

Evaluate: MTTD/MTTC/MTTR SLAs, contractual remedies, retainer response hours,
training platform, tabletop exercises, executive reporting

10 = MTTD ≤15 min, MTTC ≤1 hour, MTTR ≤4 hours — all with contractual penalties.
     Dedicated IR retainer with guaranteed response hours. Security awareness
     training platform included. Annual tabletop included. Quarterly executive briefing.

7-9 = SLAs documented with penalties. IR retainer available. Training platform
     available as add-on. Tabletop facilitation available annually.

4-6 = SLAs present but without contractual penalties (best-effort only).
     IR retainer available at additional cost. Training not included.

1-3 = SLAs vague or expressed only as averages without contractual commitment.
     IR retainer not offered. Training and tabletop not addressed.

0 = No SLA commitments. No IR retainer. No training or exercise offering.

---

ENTERPRISE READINESS & SCALE (weight: 0.05)
Dimension identifier: enterprise_readiness

Evaluate: Higher education experience, peer references, multi-campus capability,
SOC uptime, key-person risk

10 = Three or more higher education references at comparable scale.
     Demonstrated multi-campus, multi-cloud capability. SOC platform uptime
     SLA ≥99.9%. Documented key-person succession plan.

7-9 = Two or more peer references. Multi-campus capability documented.
     Uptime SLA present. Minor gaps in succession planning.

4-6 = One reference from higher education or adjacent public sector.
     Limited documentation on multi-campus scale. Uptime SLA present.

1-3 = References from unrelated sectors only. Multi-campus or multi-cloud
     capability undocumented. No uptime SLA for SOC infrastructure.

0 = No references. No documented enterprise capability.

---

CONTRACTUAL RISK (weight: 0.05)
Dimension identifier: risk_level
Note: Higher score = lower contractual risk. Inverted: 10 = no material risk flags.

10 = Liability cap ≥5× annual contract value for security incidents.
     Breach notification within 24 hours. Full indemnification clause.
     Home-state governing law. Annual audit right. Clean data return/destruction terms.

7-9 = Liability cap reasonable (2-4× annual contract value). Breach notification
     within 48 hours. Indemnification present. Governing law acceptable.

4-6 = Liability cap below 2× annual value or capped at a nominal dollar amount.
     Breach notification within 72 hours. Indemnification partial.

1-3 = Liability cap at or below one year's contract value. Breach notification
     exceeds 72 hours or not specified. Governing law unfavorable (out of state).

0 = No liability cap, no indemnification, no breach notification commitment.
