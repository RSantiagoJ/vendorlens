VENDORLENS RFP EVALUATION CRITERIA
Source: Higher Education Managed Cybersecurity Services RFP
Scope: Enterprise security operations, threat detection, and incident response services

EVALUATION DIMENSIONS AND WEIGHTS
Scale: each dimension is scored 0–10. The weighted average is the overall score.
Weights sum to exactly 1.00, so the maximum possible overall score is 10.0.

STATED PRIORITIES FROM RFP

"The University requires a managed security partner capable of providing 24x7
threat detection, incident response, and security operations across a hybrid
cloud and on-premises environment spanning all academic and administrative units."

"Vendors must demonstrate current, audited security certifications and must
provide documented incident response SLAs with contractual remedies for breach."

"Total cost of ownership must be fully disclosed, including retainer fees,
per-incident costs, and costs for forensic investigation services."

CRITICAL (REQUIRED) FUNCTIONALITY — RFP Section 4

CORE SECURITY CAPABILITY (core_lms_functionality):
- 24x7 Security Operations Center (SOC) with staffed analyst tiers
- SIEM platform with real-time log ingestion and correlation rules
- Threat detection: network anomaly, endpoint behavioral, identity-based
- Vulnerability scanning — authenticated, unauthenticated, and cloud asset coverage
- Penetration testing: annual external, biennial internal, scoped on request
- Threat intelligence feeds integrated into detection pipeline
- Malware analysis and reverse engineering capability
- Phishing simulation and campaign management platform
- Dark web monitoring for credential exposure

REGULATORY FRAMEWORK COMPLIANCE (accessibility_compliance):
- SOC 2 Type II certification (current; issued within 18 months)
- ISO 27001 certification preferred
- FedRAMP authorization (Moderate or High) for cloud-delivered services
- NIST CSF alignment documentation required
- FERPA, GLBA, and applicable state data privacy law compliance documentation
- Annual third-party penetration test summary with remediation evidence

INTEGRATION & INTEROPERABILITY (integration_interoperability):
- Integration with existing SIEM/SOAR platform (Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel, or equivalent)
- Identity and access management integration (Active Directory, Azure AD, Okta)
- Ticketing system integration (ServiceNow, Jira)
- Cloud platform coverage: AWS, Azure, GCP
- API access for custom automation and reporting pipelines
- EDR/XDR platform interoperability (CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, or equivalent)

PRICING & COST TRANSPARENCY (pricing_and_licensing):
- Annual retainer fee with full scope of included services
- Per-incident response and forensic investigation pricing disclosed
- Pricing for add-on services: red team exercises, tabletop simulations, DLP
- Multi-year pricing with escalation clauses fully disclosed
- Cost for additional log sources or endpoints beyond base tier

VENDOR SECURITY POSTURE (security_and_compliance):
- Vendor's own SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certification
- Vendor's internal penetration test cadence and most recent findings summary
- Bug bounty program or responsible disclosure policy
- Supply chain security controls and third-party risk management program
- Data residency and sovereignty controls for University data processed by vendor

INCIDENT RESPONSE SLA & TRAINING (support_and_training):
- Mean time to detect (MTTD) SLA with contractual penalty
- Mean time to contain (MTTC) SLA with contractual penalty
- Mean time to remediate (MTTR) SLA with contractual penalty
- Dedicated incident response retainer with guaranteed response hours
- Security awareness training platform included or available as add-on
- Tabletop exercise facilitation (minimum annual)
- Quarterly threat briefing and executive reporting

ENTERPRISE READINESS & SCALE (enterprise_readiness):
- Track record with higher education or public sector clients at similar scale
- At least three peer institution references
- Documented capacity for multi-campus, multi-cloud environments
- Uptime SLA for SOC platform and monitoring infrastructure (target: 99.9%)
- Succession and key-person risk mitigation plan

CONTRACTUAL RISK (risk_level):
- Liability cap of at least five times annual contract value for security incidents
- Data breach notification within 24 hours of discovery
- Indemnification clause covering third-party claims arising from vendor negligence
- Governing law: institution's home state
- Right to audit vendor security controls annually
- Data return and destruction obligations at contract termination

EVALUATION PROCESS CONTEXT

VendorLens handles the written proposal evaluation phase. The committee
also conducts: technical demonstrations, proof-of-concept testing with
institutional log sources, and reference interviews with peer institutions.
