HIGHER EDUCATION CYBERSECURITY VENDOR EVALUATION POLICY — VENDORLENS RISK AGENT

SECTION 1: SECURITY VENDOR MANAGEMENT

All cybersecurity service vendors must complete a full security risk assessment before
contract execution. The University Security Team reviews each vendor to confirm:

- Vendor's own security posture meets or exceeds the services they claim to provide
- No conflict of interest between vendor's tooling and University's existing stack
- Vendor demonstrates active security research and threat intelligence capabilities
- Subcontractor and supply chain risks are disclosed and managed

The University CISO reviews:
- Security certification type, currency, and audit scope
- Incident response capability evidence (case studies, tabletop results)
- Data handling practices for University log and event data
- Personnel background check requirements for analysts with University data access

All of the following teams must provide final approval before contract execution:
- University Legal Team: liability, indemnification, and governing law review
- University Finance Team: spend authorization and budget alignment
- University CISO: security posture, integration feasibility, and SLA adequacy

SECTION 2: SECURITY CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

HIGH RISK: Vendor does not hold a current SOC 2 Type II certification.
- SOC 2 Type II (current, issued within 18 months of proposal date) is required.
- SOC 2 Type I is not acceptable as a substitute for Type II.
- ISO 27001 certification is required or must have a documented roadmap to achieve it within 12 months.
- FedRAMP Moderate or High authorization is required for any cloud-delivered services
  processing University data. FedRAMP In Process status requires written risk acceptance from CISO.
- Expired or aging certifications (>18 months) constitute a HIGH risk finding.

SECTION 3: INCIDENT RESPONSE SLA REQUIREMENTS

HIGH RISK: Vendor does not provide contractual SLA commitments with financial penalties.
- Mean Time to Detect (MTTD): must be ≤15 minutes for critical events; SLA must carry
  financial penalty for breach.
- Mean Time to Contain (MTTC): must be ≤1 hour for critical incidents.
- Mean Time to Remediate (MTTR): must be ≤4 hours for critical incidents.
- Incident response retainer with guaranteed response hours is required; best-effort-only
  commitments are not acceptable for institutional-level risk.
- Breach notification to the University must occur within 24 hours of discovery.
  Notification timelines exceeding 48 hours constitute a HIGH risk finding.

SECTION 4: DATA HANDLING AND RESIDENCY

HIGH RISK: Institutional data processed or stored outside the United States without CISO approval.
- All institutional log data, event data, and security telemetry must be processed and stored
  within the United States, or vendor must provide a documented data residency exception
  approved in writing by the CISO.
- Vendor must not use institutional security event data for product benchmarking, model training,
  or competitive intelligence without explicit written consent from the institution's CIO.
- Data return: upon contract termination, vendor must return all institutional data within 30 days
  in a portable, standard format. Data destruction must be certified within 60 days.

SECTION 5: VENDOR SECURITY POSTURE

HIGH RISK: Vendor does not hold current security certifications for their own operations.
- The vendor must hold a current SOC 2 Type II and/or ISO 27001 certification for their own
  service delivery infrastructure, not only for customer-facing components.
- Vendor must provide evidence of annual penetration testing of their own platform, with a
  summary of findings and remediation status available upon request.
- Vendor must operate a bug bounty or responsible disclosure program.
- Supply chain security: vendor must disclose all subprocessors and major subcontractors
  who have access to institutional data. Undisclosed subcontractors constitute a HIGH risk finding.

SECTION 6: LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

HIGH RISK: Liability cap below three times the annual contract value.
- The University requires a liability cap of at least five times the annual contract value
  for losses arising from security incidents attributable to vendor negligence or failure.
- Liability cap below two times the annual contract value is a HIGH risk finding.
- Vendor must provide a full indemnification clause covering third-party claims arising
  from vendor negligence, data breach, or failure to meet SLA commitments.
- The University will not accept limitations on consequential damages arising from
  security incidents or data breaches.

SECTION 7: GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

HIGH RISK: Governing law outside the institution's home state.
- All contracts must be governed by the laws of the institution's home state.
- Governing law in another U.S. state requires written approval from the institution's Legal team.
- Mandatory arbitration clauses that limit the institution's right to seek injunctive
  relief constitute a HIGH risk finding.
- Venue for dispute resolution must be in the institution's home state.

SECTION 8: RENEWAL AND TERMINATION

MEDIUM RISK: Auto-renewal with opt-out notice period exceeding 60 days.
- The University requires a termination-for-convenience clause with ≤60 days notice.
- Auto-renewal provisions with opt-out notice periods exceeding 60 days are disfavored.
- Early termination penalties exceeding three months of contract value constitute a
  MEDIUM risk finding.
- Upon notice of termination, vendor must continue service at full capacity for the
  full notice period without degradation.

SECTION 9: PERSONNEL AND BACKGROUND CHECKS

HIGH RISK: Vendor does not conduct background checks on analysts with institutional data access.
- All vendor personnel with access to institutional security data or systems must undergo
  background checks consistent with the institution's HR standards.
- Vendor must disclose the use of offshore or third-party subcontracted analysts.
  Offshore analyst access to institutional data requires CISO approval.
- Vendor must provide a key-person risk mitigation plan identifying backups for
  critical roles (lead analyst, incident commander, account manager).

SECTION 10: AUDIT AND ASSESSMENT RIGHTS

MEDIUM RISK: Vendor does not grant the University a right to audit security controls.
- The University requires the right to audit vendor security controls annually,
  either directly or through a mutually agreed-upon third party.
- Vendor must cooperate with University security assessments within 30 days of request.
- Vendor must notify the University within 24 hours of any material change to their
  security certifications, ownership, or control environment.
