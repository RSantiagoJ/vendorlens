VENDORLENS SCORING RUBRIC
Source: Higher Education Payroll Processing Services RFP
Scale: 0–10 per dimension. All scores rounded to one decimal place.
Weights sum to 1.00. Maximum possible overall score is 10.0.

Overall score = weighted sum of all dimension scores × their weights (defined in rfp_criteria_payroll.txt).

---

CORE PAYROLL FUNCTIONALITY (weight: 0.25)
Dimension identifier: core_lms_functionality

Evaluate: payroll processing for all employee classifications and pay frequencies,
garnishment processing, direct deposit and check generation, off-cycle and correction
workflows, employee self-service portal, mobile app feature parity

10 = All employee classifications and pay frequencies fully supported and documented.
     Garnishment processing (child support, levies, creditor, student loans) fully automated.
     Direct deposit to multiple accounts; same-day off-cycle processing available.
     Employee self-service: full feature parity on iOS and Android.
     Historical pay stubs and W-2s accessible for 7+ years. Retroactive pay automated.
     Positive pay, on-demand pay, and paper check all supported without add-on cost.

7-9 = Core payroll for all classifications documented. Most required features present.
      Minor gaps (e.g., on-demand pay as add-on, limited garnishment automation,
      or mobile app lacks some self-service features available on desktop).

4-6 = Core payroll processing for standard employee types documented. Notable gaps:
      graduate assistants or student workers require manual workaround, garnishment
      semi-automated, mobile app read-only or limited, retroactive pay requires manual
      calculation. Off-cycle processing requires 24-hour advance notice.

1-3 = Minimal payroll capability for non-standard employee types. Major workflows
      (garnishments, retroactive pay) require professional services or manual processing.
      No mobile self-service. Off-cycle processing not available or very costly.

0 = Core payroll processing underdocumented or absent for key employee classifications.

TAX & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE (weight: 0.15)
Dimension identifier: accessibility_compliance

Evaluate: federal and multi-state payroll tax filing accuracy and timeliness,
W-2 and 1099-NEC/MISC generation and SSA filing, ACA 1095-C processing,
new hire reporting, vendor liability for filing penalties, FLSA and garnishment compliance

10 = Full multi-state tax filing and remittance for all states. Vendor explicitly accepts
     liability for all IRS and state penalties arising from vendor-caused errors.
     W-2, 1099-NEC/MISC, and 1095-C filed on time with penalty guarantee.
     New hire reporting to all states automated. FLSA compliance tools included.
     Certified payroll reporting for Davis-Bacon projects supported.
     Foreign national and tax treaty support documented.

7-9 = Multi-state filing fully supported. Vendor accepts liability for filing penalties.
      W-2, 1099, and 1095-C supported. New hire reporting automated.
      Minor gaps (e.g., certified payroll requires add-on, limited foreign national support).

4-6 = Multi-state tax filing supported but liability for penalties is shared or limited.
      W-2 and 1099 supported. ACA 1095-C available as add-on. New hire reporting
      requires manual initiation in some states. FLSA tools basic.

1-3 = Tax filing limited to major federal forms. State filing requires manual submission.
      Vendor does not accept penalty liability. ACA support absent or extra cost.
      New hire reporting not automated.

0 = Tax filing not included or grossly underdocumented. No penalty liability accepted.

INTEGRATION & INTEROPERABILITY (weight: 0.10)
Dimension identifier: integration_interoperability

Evaluate: HRIS/HCM integration (Workday, SAP, Oracle, UKG, Ceridian), time-and-attendance
integration, GL/accounting system integration, benefits carrier data exchange,
identity management (SSO/SAML), open API for custom integrations

10 = Native certified integrations with all major HRIS platforms. Time-and-attendance
     integration fully automated (no manual import). GL integration with chart-of-accounts
     mapping and cost center allocation. Benefits carrier EDI exchange automated.
     SSO via SAML 2.0 supported. Open REST API with full documentation.
     Positive pay file auto-generated and transmitted to bank.

7-9 = Integrates with most major HRIS platforms. Time integration automated.
      GL integration supported. Benefits EDI available. API documented.
      Minor gaps (e.g., one HRIS requires middleware, SSO limited to one IdP).

4-6 = HRIS integration via flat-file import/export (manual or scheduled). Time data
      requires manual validation before payroll. GL integration via file export only.
      Benefits deduction data manually entered. API limited or read-only.

1-3 = No native HRIS integration. Time data entered manually. GL journal entries
      require manual creation. No benefits carrier exchange.

0 = No documented integrations. All payroll inputs require manual entry.

PRICING & TOTAL COST OF OWNERSHIP (weight: 0.10)
Dimension identifier: pricing_and_licensing

Evaluate: per-employee-per-month (PEPM) pricing transparency across all employee tiers,
implementation and data migration costs, multi-year escalation clauses,
year-end W-2 and 1099 fees, off-cycle processing costs, add-on module pricing

10 = Single PEPM rate covering all employee types and pay frequencies with no tiers.
     Implementation and data migration included or fixed-fee with ceiling. Multi-year
     pricing with escalation ≤3% annually. W-2 and 1099 processing included.
     Off-cycle processing included (unlimited or generous allowance). No per-check fees.
     No hidden charges for garnishments, tax amendments, or reprocessing.

7-9 = PEPM pricing transparent. Most costs disclosed. Minor add-ons (e.g., 1099-NEC
      at small per-form fee, additional off-cycle runs at modest cost). Escalation
      disclosed and capped. Implementation estimate provided.

4-6 = PEPM for standard employees disclosed. Non-standard types (students, casuals) at
      different tier or add-on. W-2 processing fee not included. Off-cycle charged per run.
      Escalation clause above 5% or not capped. Implementation quoted as time-and-materials.

1-3 = Pricing opaque for non-standard employee types. Multiple undisclosed fees.
      Implementation costs require separate scoping. Escalation uncapped or excessive.

0 = No PEPM pricing. No implementation cost estimate. Significant undisclosed fees.

DATA SECURITY & PRIVACY (weight: 0.15)
Dimension identifier: security_and_compliance

Evaluate: SOC 2 Type II (current) and SOC 1 Type II (SSAE 18) for payroll processing,
payroll data encryption in transit and at rest, DPA as standard (not on request),
IRS data handling compliance, data ownership explicitly with institution,
no use of employee data for product improvement or AI training

10 = SOC 2 Type II AND SOC 1 Type II (SSAE 18) current certifications provided.
     DPA as standard; addresses FERPA, GLBA, state data privacy, and IRS data handling.
     Payroll and employee data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
     Institution owns all employee and payroll data. Vendor does not use employee
     data for AI training, benchmarking, or product improvement.
     Data exportable in standard formats within 10 business days of termination.
     IRS-compliant 7-year retention with certified deletion on request.

7-9 = SOC 2 Type II current. SOC 1 Type II available (may be recent). DPA provided
      as standard. Encryption documented. Data ownership clear. Minor gaps (e.g.,
      anonymized usage data may be retained; opt-out available).

4-6 = SOC 2 Type II current but SOC 1 Type II only available on request. DPA available
      on request only. Encryption claimed but not fully documented. Data ownership
      language vague. Some anonymized data retained by vendor.

1-3 = SOC 2 Type I only or certifications aging. DPA not mentioned. Encryption not
      documented. Vendor retains data rights beyond service delivery.

0 = No certifications. No DPA. Data security practices unaddressed.

IMPLEMENTATION & ONGOING SUPPORT (weight: 0.10)
Dimension identifier: support_and_training

Evaluate: implementation methodology and timeline realism, dedicated payroll
implementation specialist, parallel payroll processing period, year-end support model,
support tiers and hours, dedicated account manager, references with successful migrations

10 = Structured implementation with dedicated payroll implementation specialist.
     Parallel payroll processing for minimum 2 pay cycles before go-live cutover.
     Named account manager and payroll support specialist included post go-live.
     Year-end (W-2/1099) dedicated support team with extended hours.
     24/7 support for payroll processing windows; business hours for general queries.
     Contractual SLA with financial penalty for payroll processing failures.
     Three higher education payroll migration references with comparable complexity.

7-9 = Dedicated implementation specialist. Parallel processing included (1-2 cycles).
      Account manager available. Year-end support documented. SLAs defined.
      References from higher education provided.

4-6 = Implementation supported but no dedicated specialist. Parallel processing
      available but limited. Year-end support at additional cost. SLAs best-effort.
      Support limited to business hours. References from unrelated sectors.

1-3 = Self-guided implementation with online resources. No parallel processing.
      No dedicated support contact. Year-end processing self-service.
      No contractual SLAs. No higher education references.

0 = No implementation support. No dedicated contact. No SLAs documented.

ENTERPRISE READINESS (weight: 0.10)
Dimension identifier: enterprise_readiness

Evaluate: multi-campus and multi-entity payroll support, multiple EIN management,
high-volume payroll processing capacity, role-based access control for payroll admins,
system availability SLA during payroll windows, disaster recovery for payroll continuity

10 = Multiple EIN and FEIN management in a single tenant. Each campus/entity with
     isolated payroll admin access and separate audit trails. Confirmed capacity for
     10,000+ employee payroll processed within standard window. RBAC at field and
     transaction level for payroll administrators. 99.9% uptime SLA for payroll
     processing windows with financial penalty. DR: RTO ≤4 hours, RPO ≤1 hour.
     Published system status and historical uptime at trust portal.

7-9 = Multi-EIN supported. Campus-level admin isolation available. High-volume capacity
      documented. RBAC present. Uptime SLA for payroll windows at 99.5%+. DR documented.

4-6 = Multi-EIN technically supported but limited admin isolation. Volume capacity
      not explicitly documented. RBAC coarse-grained. Uptime SLA without financial
      penalty. DR plan exists but RTO/RPO not specified.

1-3 = Designed for single-entity payroll. Multi-campus requires workarounds.
      No published volume capacity. No payroll-window uptime SLA. DR not documented.

0 = No multi-entity support. No payroll SLA. No disaster recovery documentation.

INNOVATION & AUTOMATION ROADMAP (weight: 0.05)
Dimension identifier: innovation_roadmap

Evaluate: AI/ML payroll anomaly detection, on-demand pay and earned wage access roadmap,
predictive labor cost analytics, automation of manual payroll tasks, self-service
enhancements, mobile capability roadmap, continuous delivery model

10 = Documented 18-24 month roadmap with committed AI/automation feature timelines.
     AI-powered anomaly detection (outlier hours, duplicate pay) already live or imminent.
     On-demand pay / earned wage access available or on near-term roadmap.
     Predictive labor cost analytics included or planned. Continuous delivery model
     with regular releases. Higher education customer advisory council influencing roadmap.

7-9 = Roadmap provided. AI anomaly detection in development or early access.
      On-demand pay available or planned. Regular release cadence documented.

4-6 = High-level roadmap. AI features aspirational; no committed timeline.
      On-demand pay not available or on distant roadmap. Infrequent major releases.

1-3 = Vague roadmap. No AI or automation plan. Platform stable but not innovating.

0 = No roadmap. Platform appears legacy or end of investment cycle.

4  = Two HIGH severity risk flags
