STATED PRIORITIES FROM RFP

"The institution requires a full-service payroll processing partner capable of
handling all employee classifications — faculty, staff, graduate assistants,
student workers, and temporary employees — across multiple pay schedules
and campus units."

"Tax filing accuracy is non-negotiable. The selected vendor must bear full
responsibility for penalties resulting from late or inaccurate federal, state,
and local tax filings."

"Seamless integration with our existing HRIS and time-and-attendance systems
is required. Manual data entry between systems is not acceptable."

"Employee self-service — including pay stub access, W-2 retrieval, direct
deposit management, and tax withholding updates — must be available on
mobile and desktop without IT assistance."

CRITICAL (REQUIRED) FUNCTIONALITY — RFP Section 4

PAYROLL PROCESSING:
- Multi-frequency payroll: weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, monthly, and off-cycle runs
- All employee classifications: exempt, non-exempt, faculty (9/10/12-month contracts),
  graduate assistants, student workers, casual/temporary
- Multiple pay rates and positions per employee
- Retroactive pay calculations and adjustments
- Shift differentials, hazard pay, and special compensation types
- On-demand pay / earned wage access (preferred)
- Direct deposit to multiple accounts; paper check generation
- Positive pay file generation for institutional bank
- Garnishment processing: child support, tax levies, creditor garnishments, student loans
- Off-cycle and manual check processing within same-day window
- Payroll reversal and correction workflows with audit trail

TAX FILING AND COMPLIANCE:
- Federal payroll tax calculation, filing, and remittance (940, 941, 944)
- Multi-state payroll tax filing and remittance for all states where employees work
- Local payroll tax filing where applicable
- W-2 generation and electronic filing with SSA by January 31
- 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC for non-employee compensation
- ACA 1095-C generation and filing (institutions with 50+ FTE)
- New hire reporting to all required state agencies
- Vendor bears all IRS and state penalties for errors in vendor-controlled filings

EMPLOYEE SELF-SERVICE:
- Pay stub access (current and historical, minimum 7 years)
- W-2 and 1099 electronic delivery and download
- Direct deposit enrollment and updates (no paper form required)
- Federal and state tax withholding updates (W-4)
- Personal information updates (address, emergency contact)
- Mobile application: iOS and Android, full feature parity with web portal
- Accessible to employees without institutional email (for alumni, retirees, separated employees)

PREFERRED FUNCTIONALITY — RFP Section 4

- On-demand pay / earned wage access integrated with payroll
- AI-powered payroll anomaly detection (flags outlier hours, duplicate pay, sudden changes)
- Predictive payroll analytics and labor cost forecasting
- Benefits deduction reconciliation against carrier invoices
- Automated new-hire checklist and onboarding workflow
- Certified payroll reporting for grant-funded or Davis-Bacon projects
- Retiree and COBRA payment processing
- International payroll support for foreign national employees and treaty exemptions

REQUIRED PROPOSAL DELIVERABLES — RFP Section 6

- Per-employee per-month (PEPM) pricing for all employee tiers
- Implementation and data migration cost estimate
- Multi-year pricing with escalation clauses fully disclosed
- Tax filing liability statement: confirm vendor bears penalty responsibility
- SOC 2 Type II and SOC 1 Type II (SSAE 18) current certifications
- DPA template addressing FERPA, applicable state data privacy laws, and IRS data handling
- Integration documentation: HRIS, time-and-attendance, GL/accounting system
- Payroll processing SLA with financial penalty structure
- System availability SLA and historical uptime for prior 24 months
- At least three higher education references with comparable workforce complexity
- W-2 and year-end processing timeline and support model
- Employee self-service portal demonstration and mobile app walkthrough
- Garnishment processing workflow and volume capacity documentation
- Data export documentation: formats, scope, and 10-business-day termination timeline

EVALUATION PROCESS CONTEXT

VendorLens handles the written proposal evaluation phase. The committee
also conducts: parallel payroll processing tests using anonymized sample data,
employee self-service demos, tax compliance Q&A with vendor tax specialists,
and reference calls with peer institution payroll directors.
