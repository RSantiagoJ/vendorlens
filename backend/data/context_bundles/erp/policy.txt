HIGHER EDUCATION VENDOR EVALUATION POLICY — DISTILLED RULES FOR VENDORLENS RISK AGENT
Source: Enterprise Finance & HR Platform Procurement

SECTION 1: SECURITY VENDOR MANAGEMENT

All finance and HR platform vendors must complete a security risk questionnaire
before contract execution. Review confirms:

- No duplication of existing institutional tool functionality
- Fits within the current technology ecosystem
- Supports centralized management where possible
- Allows the institution to scale without excessive friction or cost

The following teams must provide final approval before contract execution:

- Legal Team: contract documentation and applicable data privacy compliance
- Finance Team: spend against budget and TCO validation
- CISO: security posture and payroll/financial data protection
- HR Leadership: workforce data governance and payroll accuracy

SECTION 2: SECURITY CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

- SOC 2 Type II (current) OR ISO 27001 certification required
- SOC 2 Type I does not satisfy this requirement
- Certifications in progress do not satisfy this requirement
- PCI DSS compliance required if vendor processes payment card transactions

SECTION 3: DATA PROCESSING AND GOVERNANCE

- A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is required before contract execution
- DPA must address: FERPA (student financial records), GLBA, applicable state data
  privacy laws, IRS data handling requirements, and payroll data confidentiality
- Institution retains full ownership of all financial, HR, and payroll data at all times
- Vendor may not use institutional data for any purpose beyond direct service
  delivery without explicit CIO approval
- All data must be exportable in standard formats at no cost within 10 business
  days of contract termination
- Payroll and benefits data must be encrypted in transit and at rest

SECTION 4: CONTRACT TERM REQUIREMENTS

- Termination for convenience: maximum 90 days notice, no financial penalty
- Early termination fees not permitted
- Liability cap minimum: $1,000,000 (elevated due to payroll/financial data sensitivity)
- IP ownership of configurations, custom reports, and workflow definitions must
  remain with the institution
- Vendors may not modify terms or pricing unilaterally
- Governing law: institution's home state
- Auto-renewal opt-out: minimum 60 days notice required
- Implementation cost overruns exceeding 20% of contracted amount require
  written approval and revised scope agreement

SECTION 5: SERVICE LEVEL REQUIREMENTS

- Minimum uptime guarantee: 99.9% for payroll processing windows; 99.5% general
- Payroll processing must complete within contracted SLA windows
- Critical financial period close support (month-end, year-end) must have
  dedicated support coverage with defined escalation path
- Support response times must be contractually defined with financial penalties

SECTION 6: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All configurations, custom reports, integrations, and workflows developed during
the contract and paid for with institutional funds vest with the institution at
termination. Vendor retains rights to base platform IP only.

SECTION 7: REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

- IRS reporting obligations (W-2, 1099, ACA 1095-C) must be supported natively
- GASB accounting standards compliance required for public institutions
- Federal grant management must comply with 2 CFR 200 (Uniform Guidance)
- Must support NACUBO reporting requirements
- HIPAA-adjacent protections required for any benefits or health-adjacent data

SECTION 8: ASSIGNMENT AND DELEGATION

- Contractor may not assign or transfer contract without prior written consent
- Subcontractors processing payroll or financial data require prior written approval
  and must meet the same security certification requirements

SECTION 9: CHOICE OF LAW

- Governed by laws of the institution's home state
- Without giving effect to conflicts of law principles

SECTION 10: HIGH SEVERITY RISK TRIGGERS

The following conditions require CISO, CFO, or VP-level approval and are
contract blockers:

- No Data Processing Agreement (DPA) provided or mentioned
- No current SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification
- No Incident Response or Disaster Recovery documentation
- Vendor use of payroll, financial, or HR data beyond service delivery
- Institution data not explicitly owned by the institution
- Data not exportable in standard formats at no cost upon termination
- IP ownership of configurations or custom work product transferred to vendor
- Vendor right to modify pricing or terms unilaterally
- Liability cap below $1,000,000 or not specified
- Auto-renewal opt-out window shorter than 60 days
- Early termination fee or penalty present
- Governing law not institution's home state
- No contractual SLA for payroll processing windows
- Implementation cost overrun terms not addressed
