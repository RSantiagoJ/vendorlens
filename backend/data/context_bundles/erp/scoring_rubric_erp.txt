VENDORLENS SCORING RUBRIC
Source: Higher Education Enterprise Finance & HR Platform (ERP) RFP
Scale: 0–10 per dimension. All scores rounded to one decimal place.
Weights sum to 1.00. Maximum possible overall score is 10.0.

Overall score = weighted sum of all dimension scores × their weights (defined in rfp_criteria_erp.txt).

---

CORE ERP FUNCTIONALITY (weight: 0.25)
Dimension identifier: core_lms_functionality

Evaluate: financial management (GL, AP, AR, budgeting, grants, procurement),
HR and payroll (full employee lifecycle, benefits, IRS reporting, time and attendance,
faculty workflows), and required features documented vs. add-on or absent

10 = All critical financial and HR/payroll functionality fully documented and included
     in base price. Multi-fund accounting, GASB compliance, and 2 CFR 200 grant management
     native. Full payroll for all employee types. IRS reporting (W-2, 1099, ACA) native.
     Faculty-specific workflows (tenure, effort reporting) included. Self-service portal
     and manager dashboards included. All required proposal deliverables present.

7-9 = Core financials and payroll fully documented. Minor gaps in preferred features
      (e.g., advanced grant pre-award as add-on, faculty effort reporting limited,
      or endowment tracking requires configuration). Most required deliverables present.

4-6 = Core GL and payroll documented. Notable gaps: grant management requires add-on
      or professional services, benefits administration basic, faculty workflows absent,
      or IRS reporting requires third-party integration. Several required deliverables missing.

1-3 = Minimal functionality documented. Major modules (grants, benefits, time and attendance)
      absent or vaguely described. No faculty-specific workflows. Limited reporting.

0 = Core financial or payroll capability absent or critically underdocumented.

REGULATORY & STANDARDS COMPLIANCE (weight: 0.05)
Dimension identifier: accessibility_compliance

Evaluate: GASB accounting standards, 2 CFR 200 / Uniform Guidance grant compliance,
IRS reporting obligations (W-2, 1099, ACA 1095-C), NACUBO reporting,
WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility for employee-facing portals, Section 508 conformance

10 = GASB compliance fully documented with reference implementation at comparable
     institution. 2 CFR 200 compliance certified by auditor. All IRS forms native.
     NACUBO reporting supported. WCAG 2.1 AA conformance for all employee-facing
     interfaces with current VPAT. Known accessibility gaps disclosed.

7-9 = GASB and IRS compliance documented. 2 CFR 200 supported with minor configuration
      required. Accessibility documentation present; minor gaps disclosed.

4-6 = GASB compliance claimed but not fully documented. Grant compliance requires
      add-on module. IRS reporting native for most forms. VPAT present but aging.

1-3 = Regulatory compliance vague or requires follow-up. Grant management does not
      address 2 CFR 200. IRS reporting via third-party only. No accessibility documentation.

0 = No regulatory compliance documentation. Core accounting standards not addressed.

INTEGRATION & INTEROPERABILITY (weight: 0.15)
Dimension identifier: integration_interoperability

Evaluate: SIS integration (Banner, Workday, PeopleSoft, Ellucian), LMS integration,
banking and ACH/wire transfer connectivity, benefits carrier data exchange,
identity management (SSO/SAML 2.0, Azure AD, Okta), open REST API,
document management integration

10 = Native certified integrations with all major SIS platforms. Banking integrations
     (ACH, positive pay, wire) fully documented. Benefits carrier EDI exchange native.
     SSO via SAML 2.0 with major IdPs (Azure AD, Okta, Shibboleth). Full open REST API
     with read/write access and published documentation. LMS gradebook-to-payroll
     integration for student payroll. All data exportable in standard formats.

7-9 = Integrates with most major SIS platforms. Banking integration documented.
      Benefits EDI supported. SSO present. API available with reasonable documentation.
      Minor gaps (e.g., one SIS requires middleware, LMS integration via flat-file).

4-6 = SIS integration via flat-file or basic API only. Banking integration limited
      (manual upload for some transaction types). Benefits carrier integration manual.
      SSO supported but limited IdP compatibility. API read-only or restricted scope.

1-3 = SIS integration requires significant custom development. No banking integration.
      Benefits carrier exchange manual. No published API. Data portability limited.

0 = No documented integrations, no API, data portability not addressed.

PRICING & TOTAL COST OF OWNERSHIP (weight: 0.10)
Dimension identifier: pricing_and_licensing

Evaluate: implementation cost transparency (discovery, configuration, testing, go-live,
data migration), SaaS subscription pricing per FTE or module, multi-year escalation,
hypercare and post go-live support costs, add-on module pricing, offshore/subcontractor cost

10 = Fully itemized implementation estimate with line-item costs per phase.
     Fixed-fee implementation with capped overrun exposure. SaaS pricing per FTE
     or module with all-campus total cost. Escalation ≤3% annually for full term.
     Data migration included. Hypercare and Year 1 support included. No hidden costs.

7-9 = Implementation estimate provided with major phases costed. Most costs disclosed.
      Minor gaps (e.g., data migration quoted separately, hypercare at additional cost,
      or escalation clause partially capped). Overall TCO picture reasonably clear.

4-6 = Base subscription pricing provided. Implementation quoted as time-and-materials
      without ceiling. Data migration costs not disclosed. Add-on module pricing absent.
      Escalation clause present but not capped or above 5% annually.

1-3 = Implementation costs not disclosed or described as "to be scoped post-award."
      Subscription pricing opaque for multi-campus scenarios. Major hidden cost risk.

0 = No pricing information. No implementation cost estimate. No TCO transparency.

SECURITY & DATA PRIVACY (weight: 0.15)
Dimension identifier: security_and_compliance

Evaluate: SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 (current), FERPA/GLBA/payroll data protection,
DPA standard (not on request), payroll data encryption in transit and at rest,
IR/DR documentation, PCI DSS for payment processing, data residency controls

10 = SOC 2 Type II AND/OR ISO 27001 current certificates provided with proposal.
     DPA provided as standard, explicitly addresses FERPA, GLBA, payroll confidentiality,
     and applicable state data privacy law. Payroll and financial data encrypted in
     transit and at rest (AES-256 documented). IR plan and DR documentation included.
     Institution owns all data. Vendor does not use institutional data for product
     improvement or AI training. PCI DSS certified if payment processing is in scope.
     Penetration test summary available under NDA.

7-9 = Strong security posture. Certifications current. DPA FERPA/GLBA-compliant.
      Encryption documented. Minor gaps (e.g., DR summary only, pen test timing).

4-6 = SOC 2 Type I only, or DPA available on request only. Encryption claimed but
      not documented. FERPA compliance vague. IR/DR incomplete.

1-3 = No current certifications. DPA not mentioned or non-compliant. Payroll data
      security practices unclear. No IR/DR documentation.

0 = No certifications, no DPA, data protection unaddressed.

IMPLEMENTATION & ONGOING SUPPORT (weight: 0.15)
Dimension identifier: support_and_training

Evaluate: implementation methodology and timeline realism, dedicated staffing model
(on/offshore composition, named resources), hypercare period, post go-live support SLAs,
payroll window support coverage, training and change management plan, references
with successful go-live outcomes

10 = Detailed implementation methodology with milestone schedule and dependencies.
     Fixed-fee implementation with named senior resources and certified consultants.
     On-shore or near-shore lead with offshore disclosed. Hypercare period ≥90 days
     included at no cost. Post go-live SLAs with financial penalties. 24/7 payroll
     window coverage contractually committed. Comprehensive training and change
     management plan included. Three higher education go-live references at comparable
     scope provided with verifiable contacts.

7-9 = Implementation plan and timeline provided. Most staffing disclosed. Hypercare
      included or at modest cost. Post go-live SLAs defined. References provided.
      Training included but limited in scope or duration.

4-6 = High-level implementation plan only. Staffing model not disclosed. Hypercare
      not included. Support SLAs defined without financial penalties. Training
      available at additional cost per session. References provided but limited scope.

1-3 = No implementation methodology. No staffing model. No hypercare. SLAs
      aspirational only. No training plan. References from non-comparable institutions.

0 = No implementation plan, no support SLAs, no training, no references.

ENTERPRISE READINESS (weight: 0.05)
Dimension identifier: enterprise_readiness

Evaluate: multi-campus and multi-entity support, chart of accounts flexibility,
role-based access control granularity, uptime SLA with published history,
disaster recovery RTO/RPO, sandbox and testing environment availability

10 = Full multi-entity accounting with separate and consolidated reporting.
     Unlimited chart of accounts segments with inter-entity eliminations automated.
     Granular RBAC at field and transaction level. 99.9%+ uptime SLA with published
     incident history. DR with RTO ≤4 hours and RPO ≤1 hour documented.
     Dedicated sandbox for configuration testing and year-end practice runs.

7-9 = Multi-entity support documented. Chart of accounts flexible. RBAC present.
      Uptime SLA at 99.5%+. DR documented. Sandbox available pre-contract.

4-6 = Basic multi-entity support with manual consolidation. Chart of accounts limited.
      RBAC present but coarse-grained. Uptime SLA without published history.
      Sandbox available for short trial only.

1-3 = Designed for single-entity use. Multi-campus requires custom development.
      Limited RBAC. No published uptime history. No sandbox environment.

0 = No multi-entity support, no RBAC documentation, no test environment.

INNOVATION & AUTOMATION ROADMAP (weight: 0.05)
Dimension identifier: innovation_roadmap

Evaluate: 18-24 month product roadmap, AI/ML automation plans (anomaly detection,
spend analytics, payroll reconciliation), self-service and mobile capability roadmap,
continuous delivery model, R&D investment evidence, higher education customer influence

10 = Detailed 18-24 month roadmap with committed AI/automation feature timelines.
     Explicit plans for: AI spend anomaly detection, automated payroll reconciliation,
     intelligent budget forecasting, and enhanced self-service. Continuous delivery
     model with regular release cadence documented. Higher education advisory council
     influencing roadmap. R&D investment or headcount disclosed.

7-9 = Roadmap provided for 12-18 months. AI/automation features identified with
      near-term timelines. Regular releases evident. Customer feedback mechanisms exist.

4-6 = High-level roadmap only. AI features mentioned but no committed timeline.
      Release cadence infrequent or unclear. Minimal customer input to roadmap.

1-3 = Vague or aspirational roadmap. No automation plan. Platform appears stable
      but not actively innovating. No evidence of higher education focus in roadmap.

0 = No roadmap provided. Platform appears legacy or approaching end of investment.

RISK LEVEL (weight: 0.05 — INVERTED: higher score = fewer HIGH severity risk flags)

10 = Zero HIGH severity risk flags
7  = One HIGH severity risk flag
4  = Two HIGH severity risk flags
0  = Three or more HIGH severity risk flags

Note: This dimension is directly derived from the Risk Agent output.
MEDIUM and LOW severity flags do not affect this score but are reported separately.
