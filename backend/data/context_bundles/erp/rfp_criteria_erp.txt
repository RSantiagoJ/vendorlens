Source: Higher Education Enterprise Finance & HR Platform (ERP) RFP
Scope: Financial management, HR, payroll, procurement, and grant management

STATED PRIORITIES FROM RFP

"The institution requires a modern, unified ERP platform capable of managing
financial operations, human resources, payroll, and procurement across all
campuses and administrative units in a single system of record."

"Full transparency on total cost of ownership — including implementation,
data migration, training, and integration — is required. Phased pricing
and multi-year escalation clauses must be fully disclosed."

"The selected platform must support federal grant management under 2 CFR 200,
GASB accounting standards, and all applicable IRS reporting obligations."

"Implementation methodology, timeline, and dedicated staffing must be fully
documented. References from comparable higher education institutions with
successful go-live outcomes are required."

CRITICAL (REQUIRED) FUNCTIONALITY — RFP Section 4

FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT:
- General ledger with full chart of accounts flexibility
- Accounts payable and accounts receivable
- Budget planning, forecasting, and variance reporting
- Multi-fund, multi-entity accounting (operating, capital, endowment, grants)
- Federal grant and contract management compliant with 2 CFR 200 / Uniform Guidance
- GASB-compliant financial reporting for public institutions
- Fixed asset management and depreciation tracking
- Cash management and treasury operations
- Procurement: purchase orders, vendor management, contract tracking
- Travel and expense management with policy enforcement

HUMAN RESOURCES & PAYROLL:
- Full employee lifecycle management (hire to retire)
- Position management and organizational hierarchy
- Payroll processing for all employee classifications (faculty, staff, student workers)
- Benefits administration and open enrollment
- Time and attendance tracking
- IRS reporting: W-2, 1099, ACA 1095-C generation and filing
- FMLA, ADA, and leave management tracking
- Performance management and goal-setting framework
- Compensation planning and salary administration
- Faculty-specific workflows: tenure tracking, course load, effort reporting

PREFERRED FUNCTIONALITY — RFP Section 4

- AI-powered spend analytics and budget anomaly detection
- Self-service portal for employees (pay stubs, W-2s, benefits enrollment, PTO requests)
- Manager self-service for approvals and workforce reporting
- Mobile app with approval workflows for managers and employees
- Advanced workforce analytics and headcount planning
- Research grant pre-award and post-award management
- Endowment performance reporting and distribution tracking
- Supplier diversity tracking and reporting
- Automated workflow engine for multi-step approvals
- Native document management for contracts and HR files

REQUIRED PROPOSAL DELIVERABLES — RFP Section 6

- Fully itemized implementation cost estimate (discovery, configuration, testing, go-live)
- SaaS subscription pricing per FTE or module with all-campus total cost
- Multi-year pricing with escalation clauses fully disclosed
- Data migration plan from legacy system with timeline and cost
- Integration documentation: SIS, LMS, banking, benefits carriers, identity providers
- Go-live timeline with major milestones and dependencies
- Implementation team org chart: roles, certifications, and on/offshore composition
- Hypercare and post go-live support model
- At least three higher education references with comparable scope and successful go-live
- SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification and most recent penetration test summary
- DPA template addressing FERPA, GLBA, payroll data, and applicable state law
- Uptime SLAs for payroll windows and general availability with penalty structure
- Roadmap for next 18–24 months including AI and automation features
- Subcontractor and offshore staffing disclosure

EVALUATION PROCESS CONTEXT

VendorLens handles the written proposal evaluation phase. The committee
also conducts: scripted demonstrations using institutional data scenarios,
reference calls with peer institution CFOs and CHROs, and a parallel
cost modeling exercise to validate disclosed TCO figures.
