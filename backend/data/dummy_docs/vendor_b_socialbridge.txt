VENDOR PROPOSAL — SocialBridge Solutions LLC
Submitted to: University of Massachusetts System
In Response to: Enterprise Social Media Posting and Listening Platform RFP

COMPANY OVERVIEW
SocialBridge Solutions LLC is a certified woman-owned small business (WOSB)
and minority-owned business enterprise (MBE) providing enterprise social media
management to higher education institutions since 2014. We serve 85 colleges
and universities including 12 public university systems.
Headquarters: Boston, MA. Employees: 240.

PRICING
Annual platform fee: $158,000 — fixed for full contract term
All five UMass campuses and the President's Office included in base price
No per-campus onboarding fees. No per-user overage fees up to 500 users.
All modules included: social listening, analytics, mobile app, SSO, integrations
Payment terms: Net 45, invoiced annually. 2% early payment discount available.
Billing by individual campus or business unit available at no additional cost.

CONTRACT TERMS
Contract length: 3 years with optional 2-year extension at university discretion
Auto-renewal: Does not auto-renew. Requires affirmative renewal by both parties.

Termination for cause: Immediate, full pro-rata refund of prepaid fees
Governing law: Commonwealth of Massachusetts

LIABILITY
Maximum liability: $3,000,000 or total fees paid in prior 12 months,
whichever is greater. Covers data loss, service interruption, direct damages,

PLATFORM FUNCTIONALITY

- Full posting and scheduling to all major platforms including Instagram
  carousels (supports more than 10 images), LinkedIn Groups, Facebook slideshows
- Post approval workflows with edit, approve, and reject on desktop and mobile
- Shared and campus-specific content calendars
- Campus sub-accounts: unlimited, each with isolated channels, assets, analytics
- Campus-specific asset libraries with optional cross-campus asset sharing
- Tiered user roles: System Admin, Campus Admin, Power User, Content Creator,
  Approver, Read-Only — all configurable
- Comment and message management with internal collaboration threads
- Advanced social listening: real-time sentiment analysis, keyword monitoring,
  automated crisis alerts, trend analysis, influencer tracking
- Analytics: customizable dashboards, campaign tracking, system-wide and
  campus-level reports, scheduled exports, Google UTM auto-tagging
- Link-in-bio and Instagram link page auto-generation included
- Custom shortened URLs included
- Optimal post time suggestions powered by historical engagement data
- Duplicate past posts and share assets between campus libraries
