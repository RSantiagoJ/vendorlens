VENDOR PROPOSAL — PulseMedia Inc.
Submitted to: University of Massachusetts System
In Response to: Enterprise Social Media Posting and Listening Platform RFP
Date: November 28, 2025

COMPANY OVERVIEW
PulseMedia Inc. has provided social media management solutions since 2015.
We serve over 200 clients across higher education, healthcare, and government.
Headquarters: Austin, TX. Employees: 180.

PRICING
Annual platform fee: $195,000 per year
Year 2 increase: 8% applied automatically
Year 3 increase: 8% applied automatically
Additional campus onboarding: $15,000 per campus after first three
Additional user blocks (50 users): $2,500 per block per year
Payment terms: Net 30, invoiced annually in advance, non-refundable

CONTRACT TERMS
Contract length: 3 years, non-negotiable
Auto-renewal: Automatically renews for successive 1-year terms
Opt-out notice required: 30 days prior to renewal date
Termination for convenience: 180 days written notice required plus
payment of 50% of remaining contract value as termination fee
Governing law: State of Texas

LIABILITY
Maximum liability: Limited to one month of fees paid ($16,250)
Excludes all data loss, consequential damages, and reputational harm

PLATFORM FUNCTIONALITY

- Post scheduling and publishing to major social channels
- Basic approval workflows
- Content calendar view
- Reporting dashboard (standard templates only, no custom exports)
- Comment and message management
- Social listening: keyword monitoring and basic alerts
- Sentiment analysis: available as add-on module ($18,000/year additional)
- Campus sub-accounts: up to 3 included, additional at $15,000 each
- Asset libraries: shared system-wide library only, no campus-specific libraries
- User roles: Admin and Creator only, no tiered permissions

MOBILE APPLICATION
PulseMedia offers a mobile application for iOS. Android version is in
development and expected Q3 2026. Approval workflows on mobile are
limited to review only — approvers cannot edit posts on mobile.

ACCESSIBILITY
VPAT: Available upon request. Last tested: March 2024.
Alt text support: Available for Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn.
Instagram alt text: Not currently supported via API.
Caption file support: Manual upload only, .srt not accepted.
WCAG compliance: Partial — known issues with keyboard navigation in
the content calendar module under remediation.

SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE
Security certifications: SOC 2 Type I (audit in progress for Type II)
Data Processing Agreement: Available upon request, not standard
Incident Response documentation: Available upon request
University data: PulseMedia may use anonymized engagement data for
platform benchmarking and product improvement purposes
Data ownership: PulseMedia retains rights to aggregated analytics data
Data portability: Export available in CSV format, 30-day window post-termination
Encryption: TLS in transit, AES-128 at rest

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Platform configurations created during onboarding are considered PulseMedia
proprietary templates. University retains ownership of raw content only.

SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
Uptime target: 99% (allows approximately 87 hours downtime per year)
Support response: Best effort, no contractually defined response times
No financial penalties for SLA breach
Scheduled maintenance: Up to 8 hours per month, not counted against uptime

SUPPORT AND TRAINING
Support hours: Monday-Friday 9am-5pm ET
Contact methods: Email and support portal only
Dedicated account manager: Not included in base pricing ($12,000/year add-on)
Onboarding: One recorded webinar provided. Live training at additional cost.
Training costs: On-site training $3,500/day plus travel expenses

CUSTOMER REFERENCES
Three references available upon request. One higher education client.

SANDBOX ENVIRONMENT
Available for 30 days during contract negotiation only, not post-signing.

SUPPLIER DIVERSITY
PulseMedia Inc. does not hold any supplier diversity classifications.

ADDITIONAL TERMS
PulseMedia reserves the right to modify platform features and pricing
with 30 days written notice.
University agrees not to participate in competitive benchmark studies
or publicly disclose pricing terms.
