VENDORLENS SCORING RUBRIC
Source: Derived from Higher Education Enterprise Social Media Platform RFP evaluation criteria
Scale: 0-10 per dimension. All scores rounded to one decimal place.

Overall score = weighted average of all dimensions per weights in rfp_criteria.txt

---

PLATFORM FUNCTIONALITY (weight: 0.20)

Evaluate: posting/scheduling, approval workflows, content calendar,
campus sub-accounts, asset libraries, comment management, tiered user roles,
social listening (sentiment + alerts + keyword monitoring + crisis detection),
analytics/reporting

10 = All critical RFP functionality fully documented and included in base price.
     Social listening with sentiment, crisis alerts, keyword monitoring included.
     Full approval workflows on desktop and mobile. Unlimited campus sub-accounts
     with isolated asset libraries. Tiered roles fully configurable.

7-9 = Most required functionality documented and present. Minor gaps in
      preferred features (e.g., crisis detection as add-on, limited carousel images).

4-6 = Core posting and scheduling present. Notable gaps in required features:
      missing mobile approval, limited role tiers, no campus-specific asset libraries,
      or social listening only as a paid add-on.

1-3 = Minimal functionality documented. Major required features absent or vague.

0 = Vague, missing, or critical features absent.

---

ACCESSIBILITY COMPLIANCE (weight: 0.15)

Evaluate: VPAT currency (within 1 year of proposal date), WCAG 2.1 AA compliance,
alt text support across all platforms, .srt caption file support

Federal deadline applies — non-compliance is a material risk.
VPAT must have been tested within the last year of the proposal date.

10 = Current VPAT (tested within 1 year), full WCAG 2.1 AA documented via third-party
     audit, alt text supported on all platforms where API permits (with honest disclosure
     of Instagram API limitation), .srt captions supported on all platforms.

7-9 = VPAT within 1 year, most accessibility features present. Minor gaps
      (e.g., .srt captions not supported on one platform).

4-6 = VPAT present but outdated (tested more than 1 year ago) or partial WCAG
      compliance with known issues. Alt text gaps on major platforms.

1-3 = VPAT available only on request, no third-party audit, significant
      accessibility gaps not addressed.

0 = No VPAT, no accessibility documentation, WCAG status unknown.

PRICING TRANSPARENCY (weight: 0.20)

Evaluate: licensing clarity, all-in pricing for all campuses, per-campus/user/feature
costs disclosed, multi-year cost predictability, billing by unit availability

10 = Fixed transparent pricing locked for full contract term. All campuses
     and all modules included in base price. No per-campus fees, no user overages,
     no undisclosed add-on costs. Billing by campus/unit at no additional cost.
     No year-over-year price escalation.

7-9 = Mostly transparent. Minor costs not fully disclosed upfront (e.g., small
      training add-ons, optional integrations). No major hidden overages.

4-6 = Price escalation present (3-5% per year) or notable overages for users/campuses
      not fully disclosed upfront. Billing by unit available but at extra cost.

1-3 = Significant hidden costs. Escalation clause exceeds 5%. Overages unclear.
      Pricing opaque for multi-campus scenarios.

0 = Unclear tiers, undisclosed overages, aggressive escalation, or pricing
    that makes total cost unpredictable.

SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE (weight: 0.20)

Evaluate: SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 (current), DPA standard (not on request),
IR/DR docs provided, university data ownership explicit, no vendor data use
beyond service delivery

10 = SOC 2 Type II AND/OR ISO 27001 current certificates provided with proposal.
     DPA provided as standard (not on request), addresses FERPA/GLBA/Ch.93H/Ch.66A. IR plan and DR documentation
     provided with proposal. University data exclusively owned by university.
     No vendor use of data for any purpose beyond service delivery.

7-9 = Strong security posture. Certifications current. Minor documentation gaps
      (e.g., DR summary but not full doc, or DPA on request but standard template).

4-6 = SOC 2 Type I only (Type II in progress), or DPA available on request only,
      or IR/DR docs require NDA or are incomplete. Some data governance concerns.

1-3 = No current certifications or only partial/outdated ones. DPA not mentioned.
      Data use practices unclear or concerning.

0 = No certifications, no DPA, data governance unclear, university data rights
    not addressed.

---

SUPPORT AND TRAINING (weight: 0.10)

Evaluate: support hours, contractually defined response times, dedicated CSM,
onboarding training included, training costs transparent

10 = 24/7 support for critical issues. Response times contractually defined.
     Dedicated Customer Success Manager included at no cost.
     Comprehensive onboarding training included at no additional cost.
     Self-service resources (LMS, video library) also included.

7-9 = Strong support. Most elements present. CSM included or at modest cost.
      Training included but limited scope.

4-6 = Business hours support. Response times defined but no financial penalty.
      Dedicated account manager only for onboarding period or at extra cost.
      Additional training available but at per-session cost.

1-3 = Email/ticket only. Response times not defined in contract. No dedicated
      account manager. Training not included.

0 = No defined support hours, no response time commitments, no training.

---

ENTERPRISE READINESS (weight: 0.10)

Evaluate: multi-campus architecture, campus isolation, role-based admin controls,
scalable onboarding, sandbox environment availability

10 = Fully documented multi-campus architecture. Each campus fully isolated
     with own channels, content, analytics. Role-based admin at system and campus
     level. Unlimited campus scalability. Sandbox provided immediately at no cost
     and maintained throughout contract term.

7-9 = Strong multi-campus support. Minor limitations (e.g., cap on campus count,
      sandbox only pre-signing, limited role granularity).

4-6 = Basic multi-campus support. Campus sub-accounts included but limited count.
      Sandbox available for short trial period only. Role management limited to 2-3 tiers.

1-3 = Designed primarily for single-tenant use. Multi-campus adaptation requires
      custom work. No sandbox or very limited trial access.

0 = No multi-campus architecture, no admin controls, no test environment.

RISK LEVEL (weight: 0.05 — INVERTED: higher score = fewer HIGH severity risk flags)

10 = Zero HIGH severity risk flags
7  = One HIGH severity risk flag
4  = Two HIGH severity risk flags
0  = Three or more HIGH severity risk flags

Note: This dimension is directly derived from the Risk Agent output.
MEDIUM and LOW severity flags do not affect this score but are reported separately.
