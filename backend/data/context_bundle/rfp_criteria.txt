CRITICAL (REQUIRED) FUNCTIONALITY — RFP Section 4

- Posting and scheduling to all major social media platforms
  including image uploads, hashtags, post previews
- Post approval workflows: approve, edit, or reject from desktop or mobile
- Robust analytics and metrics: individual channels + aggregated reports
- Campus sub-accounts with isolated access to own channels, content,
  asset libraries — no visibility into other campuses
- Comment and message management within the platform
- Separate asset libraries per business unit
- Transparent pricing for adding campuses, channels, users
- Calendar views of scheduled posts
- Accessibility features including alt text and .srt caption files
- Tiered user roles (power users, content creators, etc.)

ACCESSIBILITY NOTE
Alt text is a REQUIREMENT, not a preference. All social posts must be
accessible by April 24, 2026 (federal deadline). Alt text cannot be added
retroactively on most platforms. Instagram API does not currently support
alt text via API — vendors must disclose this limitation.

PREFERRED FUNCTIONALITY — RFP Section 4

- Link-in-bio and Instagram link page auto-generation
- Separate billing by business unit
- Advanced social listening: sentiment analysis, automated alerts
- Custom shortened URLs, Google UTM auto-tagging, optimal post times
- SSO authentication
- Integrations: Canva, Microsoft Teams, Dropbox, Canto
- Duplicate past posts, share assets between libraries
- Post to LinkedIn Groups
- More than 10 items in Facebook slideshows and Instagram carousels

REQUIRED PROPOSAL DELIVERABLES — RFP Section 6

- Pricing breakdown: university-wide and by campus/business unit
- A la carte pricing for additional functionality
- VPAT in HTML or accessible PDF format, tested within the last year
- At least three customer references with contact info (higher ed preferred)
- Functionality breakdown including social channels and mobile details
- Support details
- Company background: customers, years of experience, employees
- Product roadmap information
- Sample analytics report
- Training options and any additional costs
- Billing and invoice information, including per-unit billing availability
- Whether sandbox/test access is available for accessibility testing

EVALUATION PROCESS CONTEXT

also conducts: vendor demos (recorded for absent members), digital
accessibility testing, hands-on platform testing, reference checks.
