HIGHER EDUCATION VENDOR EVALUATION POLICY — DISTILLED RULES FOR VENDORLENS RISK AGENT
Derived from: Institution Security Vendor Management Policy
and Institution standard Contract for Services
Additional context: Enterprise Social Media Platform RFP requirements

SECTION 1: SECURITY VENDOR MANAGEMENT

Vendor review process:
All technology vendors must complete a security risk questionnaire before
contract execution. The University Security Team reviews each vendor to confirm:

- No duplication of existing University tool functionality
- Fits within the current University technology ecosystem
- Supports centralized management where possible
- Allows the University to scale use without excessive friction or cost

The University CISO reviews:

- Security certification type and current status
- Data Processing Agreement and potential legal issues
- Incident Response and Disaster Recovery documentation
- Integration of security best practices
- Open issues or remediation items with the vendor

All of the following teams must provide final approval before contract execution:

- University Legal Team: contract documentation and applicable state data privacy compliance
- University Finance Team: spend against budget
- University CISO: security posture

SECTION 2: SECURITY CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

- SOC 2 Type II (current) OR ISO 27001 certification required
- SOC 2 Type I does not satisfy this requirement
- Certifications in progress do not satisfy this requirement

SECTION 3: DATA PROCESSING AND GOVERNANCE

- A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is required before contract execution
- DPA must address: Massachusetts Ch. 66A, Ch. 93H, FERPA, GLBA
- University retains full ownership of all institutional data at all times
- Vendor may not use university data for any purpose beyond direct service
  delivery without explicit CIO approval
- University data must be exportable in standard formats at no cost within
  10 business days of termination

SECTION 4: CONTRACT TERM REQUIREMENTS

- Termination for convenience: maximum 90 days notice, no financial penalty
- Early termination fees not permitted
- Liability cap minimum: $500,000
- IP ownership of configurations and work product must remain with university
- Vendors may not modify terms or pricing unilaterally
- Governing law: Commonwealth of Massachusetts
- Disputes: Massachusetts state courts
- Auto-renewal opt-out: minimum 60 days notice required

SECTION 5: SERVICE LEVEL REQUIREMENTS

- Minimum uptime guarantee: 99.5% with financial penalties for breach
- Support response times must be defined in the contract

SECTION 6: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All data, reports, programs, software, equipment, and documentation paid for
with university funds vests with the university at termination. If contractor
publishes work related to contract performance, university retains a
royalty-free non-exclusive irrevocable license.

SECTION 7: CONFIDENTIALITY AND PRIVACY

- Must comply with Massachusetts General Laws Ch. 66A (personal data)
  and Ch. 93H (security breach notification)
- Contractor implements feasible safeguards for Personal Information
- Contractor must inform its employees of confidentiality obligations

SECTION 8: ASSIGNMENT AND DELEGATION

- Contractor may not assign or transfer contract without prior written
  university consent
- Contractor may not subcontract without prior written university approval
- Unauthorized assignments are void

SECTION 9: CHOICE OF LAW

- Governed by laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts
- Without giving effect to conflicts of law principles

SECTION 10: FORUM SELECTION

- State courts of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts have exclusive jurisdiction
- Contractor expressly consents to Massachusetts jurisdiction

SECTION 11: HIGH SEVERITY RISK TRIGGERS

The following conditions require CISO or VP approval and are contract blockers:

- No Data Processing Agreement (DPA) provided or mentioned
- No current SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification
  (SOC 2 Type I or "in progress" does NOT satisfy this requirement)
- No Incident Response or Disaster Recovery documentation provided
- Vendor use of university data beyond service delivery (product improvement,
  benchmarking, AI training, anonymized studies) without CIO approval
- University data not explicitly owned by the university
- Data not exportable in standard formats at no cost upon termination
- IP ownership of configurations or work product transferred to vendor
- Vendor right to modify pricing or terms unilaterally
- Liability cap below $500,000 or not specified
- Auto-renewal opt-out window shorter than 60 days
- Termination penalty or early termination fee present
- Governing law not Commonwealth of Massachusetts
- Dispute resolution not in Massachusetts courts
